([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/meta-new-mexico-cambridge-analytica-penalty.html), [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/meta-takes-9-89bn-teen-settlement-charge-as-ad-revenue-gains-26/), [247wallst](https://247wallst.com/cards/meta-q3-2026-earnings-meta-01m5g1awm0ef8g90sxmwd9b4kz))
